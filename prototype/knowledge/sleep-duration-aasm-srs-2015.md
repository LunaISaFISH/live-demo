## Recommended sleep duration for adults
Adults should sleep 7 or more hours per night on a regular basis to promote optimal health.

## Sleeping less than 7 hours
Regularly sleeping less than 7 hours per night is associated with adverse health outcomes, including weight gain and obesity, diabetes, hypertension, heart disease and stroke, depression, and increased risk of death. It is also associated with impaired immune function, increased pain, impaired performance, and more errors and accidents.

## Sleeping more than 9 hours
Sleeping more than 9 hours per night on a regular basis may be appropriate for young adults, individuals recovering from sleep debt, and individuals with illnesses. For others, it is uncertain whether sleeping more than 9 hours per night is associated with health risk.
