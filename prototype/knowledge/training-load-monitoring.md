## Session RPE training load
Session RPE quantifies training load by multiplying the person's rating of perceived exertion for the whole session, on a 0 to 10 scale, by the session duration in minutes. The product can be summed across a week to track load (Foster 2001).

## Acute:chronic workload ratio and its limits
The acute:chronic workload ratio (ACWR) compares recent training load with the load over the preceding weeks. Impellizzeri and colleagues (2020) criticise it on conceptual and statistical grounds: its causal relation to injury has not been established, the statistical properties of a ratio make it an inaccurate metric, and it is not consistently and unidirectionally related to injury risk. They conclude that there is no evidence supporting its use in training-load management or for training recommendations aimed at reducing injury risk. In this library a sudden rise in load is therefore something to describe and review, not a prediction.
