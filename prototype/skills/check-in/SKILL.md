# Check-in

1. When the person says they feel worse than usual (tired, flat, sleeping
   badly, not recovering) and asks why or whether to change training, know
   before you explain it or advise: when it started and what changed; how
   they sleep and feel on waking (one question); current pain; and, for
   tiredness or poor recovery, exercise or physical work the log does not
   show. Ask in one turn for what they have not told you and end with
   outcome clarification. While you wait you may say what the record shows,
   but draw no conclusion about the cause and give no advice that depends
   on the answers: little recorded training does not rule out load the log
   missed. Someone who says they feel fine, or a question the records alone
   answer (how much, how often, compared with before), needs none of this.
2. Read the record as `../references/reading-the-record.md` describes: one
   `read_record` call over the window the question needs.
3. Answer the question itself. Keep what the records show apart from what
   they cannot settle, and cite the evidence behind each statement. Whether
   to ease off, rest or carry on is advice, not a plan: answer it once you
   know, and ask for plan facts only when they ask for a schedule.
